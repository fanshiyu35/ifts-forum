About the Forum

The International Forum on Transport Standards (IFTS) is an independent, not-for-profit international forum organisation founded in 2012 at an assembly in Prague attended by 31 participants from 9 countries. The Forum is a dialogue and comparison platform: it develops position papers, practice notes and recommendations for voluntary adoption by its participants. IFTS does not issue standards and is not a standardisation body.

The Forum convenes once a year. Each edition combines a keynote programme, thematic working group sessions, and the adoption of Forum Conclusions recording the year's shared positions. Editions rotate among European host cities and have been held both on site and online.

Programme Council

The Programme Council sets the annual theme, reviews the keynote programme and oversees the working groups. Council members serve in a personal capacity and are drawn from research, industry and transport authorities across the participating countries.

Working Groups

WG1 · Data Interoperability

Data Interoperability

Common vocabularies, reference data structures and exchange practice for multimodal transport information.

WG2 · Performance Measurement

Performance Measurement

Comparable service quality indicators and benchmarking methods across modes, networks and countries.

WG3 · Scenario Analysis & Reporting

Scenario Analysis & Reporting

Frameworks for scenario-based evaluation, ranking of alternatives, and the stability of reported rankings.

WG4 · Workforce & Competence

Workforce & Competence

Publications

Each edition concludes with the adoption of Forum Conclusions and the release of the annual IFTS Position Paper. Position Papers summarise the state of practice discussed during the edition and record the Forum's recommendations for voluntary adoption. All papers are published on this website in the edition archive.
