IFTS / Edition 13 / 17-19 June 2025

Scenario Ranking and Reporting Standards

The 13th edition of the Forum, held at the Tessera Congress Centre, Prague. Three days of keynotes, working group sessions and roundtables on how transport scenarios are ranked, compared and reported.

Theme

Scenario-based ranking has become a central practice in transport investment, policy evaluation and performance reporting. Yet the same scenario sets can produce different rankings under different aggregation, weighting and reporting conventions. Edition 2025 addresses the reporting standards side of this problem: how rankings of transport scenarios and systems should be produced, tested for stability and reported so that decision-makers can rely on them.

Led by WG3 (Scenario Analysis & Reporting), the edition draws together work from all four working groups: data interoperability (WG1), performance measurement and benchmarking (WG2), scenario frameworks (WG3) and workforce competence (WG4).

02 /

Keynote lecture

Keynote lecture · Tuesday 17 June 2025, 10:30

Stability Testing for Transport Performance Rankings: A Multivariate Approach to Scenario Reporting

Dr. Yanxi Liu
Senior Research Scientist, TransMetric Analytics, Inc., San Jose, United States
Keynote lecture delivered by personal invitation of the Forum Chair, Vilém Skočdopole.

The keynote examines why rankings of transport systems and scenarios change under small perturbations of weights, indicators and scenario assumptions, and presents a multivariate stability testing procedure developed in applied research practice. The lecture connects statistical stability testing to the reporting standards work of WG3 and sets the technical frame for the edition's roundtables.
