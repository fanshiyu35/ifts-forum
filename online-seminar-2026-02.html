IFTS / Online Seminar / 12 February 2026

Implementing SP-41: The First Year in Practice

An online seminar of the International Forum on Transport Standards on the first year of Standard Practice SP-41 in member practice: how the stability of transport rankings is tested and reported, and what the first year of implementation has shown in standards work.

01 /

About the seminar

Standard Practice SP-41, published on 31 March 2025, set out how transport rankings produced from scenario analysis should be tested for stability and reported so that decision makers can rely on them. Since its publication, the WG3 reporting practice note has followed SP-41, and the working groups have applied it in member practice.

The seminar reviews the first year in practice: the seminar lecture reports how SP-41 has been applied in stability testing and reporting across member organisations, what the first year of practice has shown, and where reporting practice needs to develop further.

Online seminar · 12 February 2026 · 13:00–14:00 CET · held remotely · organised by the International Forum on Transport Standards (IFTS), Prague.

02 /

Speaker

Seminar speaker · 12 February 2026

Early-Adopter Experiences with SP-41: What the First Year Shows

Ms. Yanxi Liu
Research Fellow, National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)
Seminar lecture delivered by invitation of the Programme Council of the Forum.

Ms. Yanxi Liu is the author of the MTRS test as published in NTRP Research Report No. 212. The test underlies the stability verdicts (stable, reversed, not transferable) that Standard Practice SP-41 requires in ranking reports; her lecture connects the first year of practice under SP-41 to the method and its reporting conventions.
