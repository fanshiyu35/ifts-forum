Founding assembly

2012

Founding Assembly, Prague

19-20 September 2012, Prague. 31 participants from 9 countries adopted the Forum Charter establishing IFTS as an independent international forum organisation and set the agenda for the first edition in 2013.

International Forum on Transport Standards

An independent international forum organisation for transport standards practice. Founded 2012, Prague.
