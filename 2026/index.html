IFTS / Edition 14 / September 2026

Ranking Stability in Standards Practice

The 14th edition of the Forum, held in September 2026 at the Tessera Congress Centre, Prague. Keynote, working group sessions and roundtables on how the stability of transport rankings is tested and reported in standards practice.

Theme

Scenario-based ranking has become a central practice in transport investment, policy evaluation and performance reporting. Yet the same scenario sets can produce different rankings under different aggregation, weighting and reporting conventions. Edition 2026 addresses the stability side of this problem: how rankings of transport scenarios and systems should be produced, tested for stability and reported so that decision-makers can rely on them. The edition builds on Standard Practice SP-41 (September 2024), which the WG3 reporting practice note has followed since its publication.

Led by WG3 (Scenario Analysis & Reporting), the edition draws together work from all four working groups: data interoperability (WG1), performance measurement and benchmarking (WG2), scenario frameworks (WG3) and workforce competence (WG4).

02 /

Keynote lecture

Keynote lecture · September 2026

Stability Testing for Transport Performance Rankings: A Multivariate Approach to Scenario Reporting

Ms. Yanxi Liu
Research Fellow, National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)
Keynote lecture delivered by personal invitation of the Forum Chair, Vilém Skočdopole.

The keynote examines why rankings of transport systems and scenarios change under small perturbations of weights, indicators and scenario assumptions, and presents the multivariate stability testing procedure published as NTRP Research Report No. 212 (May 2024). The lecture connects statistical stability testing to the reporting standards work of WG3 and sets the technical frame for the edition's roundtables.
